What deck size should I choose?

For shoe sizes 7-9 or technical street skating, we recommend 8.0" to 8.25". For shoe sizes 9.5+ or transition and bowl riding, 8.38" to 8.75" provides better stability and foot room.

Do completes come fully assembled?

Yes! All ADOP complete skateboards arrive professionally built, tuned, and ready to skate right out of the box, with options to customize components before checkout.

What makes ADOP decks more durable than standard boards?

We use individual cold-press molding with waterproof epoxy resin instead of standard water-based glues. This ensures consistent concave, reduced warping, and unmatched crisp pop that lasts.

What is your return and warranty policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy on un-skated gear and a 90-day warranty against manufacturer defects or delamination on all deck products.
