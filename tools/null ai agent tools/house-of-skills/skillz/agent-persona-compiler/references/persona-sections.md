# Persona Sections

## Required Sections

- `Role`: Specialist identity and domain.
- `Operating Mode`: How much initiative to take.
- `Workflow`: Ordered behavior for typical tasks.
- `Constraints`: Hard limits and prohibited moves.
- `Validation`: What must be checked before final output.
- `Output Contract`: What the final answer must contain.
- `Escalation`: When to ask for approval or clarification.

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not create omnipotent roles.
- Do not grant hidden permissions.
- Do not tell the agent to conceal limitations.
- Do not remove validation duties for speed modes.
