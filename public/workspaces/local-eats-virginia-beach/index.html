Our Heritage & Craft

Forged in Saltwater
& Hardwood Timber

Founded on Pacific Avenue in Virginia Beach, Cinder & Tide was born from a desire to celebrate the rich culinary terroir of the Mid-Atlantic coast through the timeless alchemy of live wood flame.

The Virginia Coastal Pantry

Virginia’s culinary heritage is rooted where the fresh rivers of the Commonwealth meet the cold Atlantic salinity. From the oyster beds of Lynnhaven Bay to the heirloom vegetable farms of Pungo and the Eastern Shore, our region offers extraordinary natural abundance.

Rather than mask these pristine ingredients with complicated sauces or heavy butter reductions, we let wood smoke and high-radiant heat amplify their clean, sweet, and mineral notes.

"When you cook with live fire, every log you choose has a voice. White oak brings caramel; hickory brings deep smoke; pecan brings gentle nuttiness."

— Chef Marcus Vance, Executive Chef & Co-Founder
