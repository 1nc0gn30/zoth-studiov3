Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Neal's Town Center service

How do you handle elevator fobs and security at The Westin and Cosmopolitan?

Neal works directly with front-desk concierges at The Westin, Cosmopolitan, and Armada Hoffler. You can choose to have your order left securely at the front desk concierge or brought directly up to your condo door by providing entry buzzer notes at checkout.

What happens if my address is slightly outside the 1-mile perimeter?

To maintain our promise of under-15-minute deliveries with hot food, Neal strictly covers locations within 1.0 mile of Town Center Fountain Plaza. You can test your exact coordinates using the 1-Mile Validator above!

How does Neal keep hot food hot during delivery?

Our custom e-bike cargo box features multi-layer commercial-grade aerospace thermal insulation. Because total transit distance across the plaza is under 1,500 yards, your food arrives steaming hot as if it just walked out of the kitchen.

Can I order from multiple plaza restaurants in the same run?

Yes! Because everything is concentrated within Town Center Plaza, Neal can bundle your Yard House burger with a Cheesecake Factory dessert in one swift dispatch run.
