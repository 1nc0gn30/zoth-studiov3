The Blueprint & Influence

The Hip-Hop Family Tree

KEY! is the vital bridge connecting underground eras. Click any node to explore how FatManKey collaborated, co-wrote, or shaped the sound of rap titans.

KB
Kenny Beats 777 Producer & Tag Origin

KEY! coined the world-famous "Whoa, Kenny!" producer tag during an early studio session. Together they created 777, cementing Kenny's status as hip-hop's go-to beatmaker.

777 LP "Whoa Kenny!" The Cave
PC
Playboi Carti Early Atlanta Wave

Ran in early Atlanta circles with Two-9 and Awful Records. KEY! and Carti traded tracks like "Ghost" and "Pump Fake", influencing the minimalist vocal delivery of modern trap.

Ghost Atlanta 2015 Vocal Flow
A$AP
A$AP Mob & Rocky Cozy Tapes Legend

Key featured prominently on A$AP Mob's legendary Cozy Tapes Vol. 1: Friends- ("Crazy Brazy") and Cozy Tapes Vol. 2 ("Telephone Calls"), representing Atlanta lyricism in NYC.

Crazy Brazy Telephone Calls AWGE
FR
Father & Awful Records "Look At Wrist" Era

Co-created the monumental 2014 viral anthem "Look At Wrist" with Father and iLoveMakonnen. The track sparked the lo-fi SoundCloud trap wave that reshaped Billboard rap.

Look At Wrist Awful Records 2014 Genesis
TS
Tony Seltzer The Alpha Jerk LP

Partnered with Brooklyn underground heavyweight Tony Seltzer in 2021 to create The Alpha Jerk, fusing abrasive NYC drill textures with Atlanta flows.

The Alpha Jerk Fallacy Brooklyn x ATL
6L
6LACK Love On Ice

Fellow East Atlanta native 6LACK joined KEY! for "Love On Ice", one of the most celebrated and atmospheric tracks on 777.

Love On Ice East Atlanta LVRN
29
Two-9 Collective Founding Co-Leader

Co-founded in 2009 alongside Curtis Williams, Reese LaFlare, Jace, and Ceej. Two-9 defined the youth movement in Atlanta during the early DatPiff golden era.

Two-9 Forever Reese LaFlare Curtis Williams
SK
Skepta London x Atlanta Bridge

Collaborated on tracks like "Red Roses" and joined Skepta's European tours, establishing a direct transatlantic connection between Grime and Atlanta Trap.

Red Roses Boy Better Know UK Grime Link
