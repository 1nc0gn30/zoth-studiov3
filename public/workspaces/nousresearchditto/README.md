# NOUS RESEARCH — Appreciation Platform & Canonical Mockup

An ultra-clean, high-fidelity dark-themed appreciation mockup honoring **Nous Research** and replicating the exact aesthetic of [nousresearch.com](https://nousresearch.com/).

## Style Directives & Aesthetics
- **Ultra-clean Dark Theme**: Deep black base (`#050608`), layered card elevations (`#0c0e14`), subtle grid background.
- **High-Contrast White Typography**: Pure white headers (`#ffffff`), muted technical subtext (`#9aa2b1`), electric blue and emerald indicator highlights.
- **Crisp Hairline Borders**: 1px structured grid dividers (`#1b202c`), hairline borders, and geometric alignment.
- **Lucide Icon Minimalism**: Minimalist vector icons with 1.5px/2px stroke weights.
- **Smooth Fluid Transitions**: Custom cubic-bezier easings (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`) for all interactive elements.
