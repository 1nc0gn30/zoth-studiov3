🏡 The Rescue Roadmap

The Adoption Journey & The 3-3-3 Rule

A compassionate, trauma-informed guide to transitioning shelter pets and creating multi-species harmony.

3 Days

Phase 1: Deep Decompression & Sensory Overload Relief

The pet is overwhelmed, terrified, and releasing shelter cortisol. They may refuse food, hide under sofas, sleep for 18 hours, or exhibit nervous pacing. Goal: Zero visitors, minimal handling, calm room sanctuary, soft classical music, and predictable quiet meals.

3 Weeks

Phase 2: Learning Daily Routines & True Personality Reveal

The pet realizes they are not going back to the shelter. Guard is lowered, true energy levels emerge, and testing of boundaries begins. Goal: Establish ironclad consistent schedules for feeding, bathroom breaks, and gentle positive training cues.

3 Months

Phase 3: Sacred Trust, Family Bond & True Home

The animal feels completely safe, secure, and bonded. Attachment is solid, quirks are integrated, and unconditional love blossoms into lifelong family devotion.

Multi-Species Integration

How to Introduce a Cat and a Dog Safely

Stage 1: Scent Swap

Keep in separate rooms. Rub towels on each pet and place under the other's food bowl so positive eating is paired with the scent.

Stage 2: Visual Barrier

Introduce through a sturdy tall baby gate. Feed treats on opposite sides when both remain calm and non-fixated.

Stage 3: Leashed Coexistence

Dog on loose leash, cat free with high escape vertical routes (cat tree). Reward dog for looking away from cat.
