## ⚡ Overview & Key Capabilities

**Zoth** is the flagship application powering the entire **Zoth Studio** ecosystem.

### Core Architecture:
- 💻 **`zoth` CLI v3.2 Sovereign Master Edition:** High-speed terminal engine (`~/.local/bin/zoth`), 60 FPS matrix digital rain screensaver (7 themes, 6 glyphs), and fullscreen 9-tab interactive curses cockpit (`zoth tui`).
- 🪐 **12-Formula #つぶやきProcessing Topology Lab:** 280-byte mathematical carrier equations for toroids, gyroids, Cassini ovals, strange attractors, and quantum spirals rendered in terminal ASCII and 3D WebGPU.
- 🐾 **24 Sovereign Mascot Spirits & Pet Dex:** Companion familiars with multi-frame ASCII sprites, soul lore, summon binds, and terminal tamagotchi interaction (`zoth pet`).
- 🧠 **Memory Whitespace & Lucy Oracle (`:8788`):** Dual-layer cognitive stratum offering human narrative story digests alongside lossless machine XML `<memory_context>`, STDP associative links, and Lucy from *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners* 3D Cyberspace.
